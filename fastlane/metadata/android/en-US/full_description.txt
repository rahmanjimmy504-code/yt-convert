YT Convert saves YouTube videos and audio straight to your phone, doing the
conversion entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded, and no server ever
sees what you download.

<b>What it does</b>

• Download any YouTube video as a progressive MP4, in the quality you pick.
• Save audio as M4A (the original AAC track, untouched) or transcode it on your
  phone to MP3, WAV, FLAC or Opus.
• For high-definition videos the app stream-copies the compatible H.264 video
  and AAC audio tracks into a single MP4 with no re-encode and no intermediate
  file.
• Downloads run as a background service with a progress notification, survive
  the screen turning off, and can be cancelled at any time.
• Finished files are saved into your Downloads / MediaStore so every other app
  can see them.

<b>Privacy, stated plainly</b>

• No account, no sign-in, no tracking and no advertising.
• No secrets, cookies or tokens are collected or sent; the app talks to YouTube
  over its public interface exactly like a normal player.
• Every stream address is checked against a strict allow-list before anything
  is fetched.

<b>Requirements and honesty</b>

MP3 encoding uses the bundled LAME encoder (LGPL). FLAC and Opus output need
Android 12+ and Android 10+ respectively; on older devices those choices are
disabled with the reason shown, and M4A/MP3 remain available.

This app downloads media for your personal use. Respect copyright and YouTube's
terms of service when you use it.
